# SSS 2 — scheme of work

NEW NERDC scheme, 2025 · 18 subjects, one table per term. Weeks in *italics* are the term's own non-teaching weeks (midterm, break, revision, the exam), kept because they are part of the term's shape.

**680 weeks.**

## English Language

3 terms · 40 weeks · 23 teaching weeks

### First term

| Week | Topic | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grammar, Oral, Summary, Vocabulary | Grammar: Nominalization of adjectives and verbs – meaning, process, conversion into nouns. | 2 | Oral, Vocabulary/Comprehension, Writing, Vocabulary | Oral: Consonant clusters (two, three, four, five consonants).
